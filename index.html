What it draws

Every member type produces a complete sheet, not a sketch: plan, sections, dimensioned bar callouts and the schedules that go with them.

Framing plan

The grid the members sit on — bays, spans, storey heights, slab edges and openings.

Footings

Pad, combined, strap and wall footings, with bar layouts and dowel detailing.

Columns

Longitudinal bars, ties and hoops, with the section schedule and splice zones.

Continuous beams

Top and bottom bars through the spans, stirrup zoning, cut-off and lap positions.

Slab panels

Two-way and one-way panels with strip reinforcement and edge conditions.

Stair flights

Flight geometry, landings, main and distribution bars in section.

Steel roof trusses

Chord and web members to AISC 360-16, with joints and member schedules.

Schedules & tables

Bar bending schedules, hook tables and lap-splice tables, generated from the same detail.

How it works

The panel is deliberately narrow so it docks beside AutoCAD rather than covering it. The drawing opens as its own window — park it on a second monitor and leave it open while you work.

  1. ConnectJI-DETAILER attaches to the AutoCAD session already open on your desk. It draws into that drawing; nothing is exported or re-imported.
  2. Set the memberPick the member type from the rail and fill in the parameters. The drawing window tracks the form as you type, so you see the detail before anything is written.
  3. CheckThe code verdict sits in the footer. Click it for the full finding list — every check, with the clause it comes from.
  4. DrawPress Draw and the finished sheet lands in the drawing on JI-DETAILER's own layers, ready to plot or fold into your sheet set.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • AutoCAD 2018 or later. JI-DETAILER drives it over ActiveX, and must run at the same privilege level as AutoCAD.
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime. The setup checks for it and links to it if it is missing.

Each release carries a SHA256SUMS file. To confirm the installer you downloaded is the one that was published: 

Get-FileHash .\JI-DETAILER-Setup-1.1.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256

Licensing

JI-DETAILER uses offline, single-PC licences. A licence is a small signed file bound to one PC identifier — there is no licence server, and the app never phones home.

  1. Install and launchAn unlicensed copy opens on an activation screen showing that PC's ID, and nothing else.
  2. Send the PC IDPress Copy on that screen and send it to your supplier.
  3. ActivatePaste the licence you get back into the same screen. The app starts immediately — there is no restart. A PDF certificate comes with it, recording the term, the licence ID and the registered PC.

Upgrading is the same installer over the top. Your licence lives under %LOCALAPPDATA%\JI-DETAILER and is deliberately left alone, so an upgrade never asks you to re-license the same PC. If a PC ID changes — a replaced motherboard or disk, or a Windows reinstall — ask for a replacement licence against the new ID.
